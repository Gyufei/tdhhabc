What is Tadle?

Tadle is the ultimate TGE-as-a-Service (TaaS) platform, redefining how projects and users interact with points, rewards, and predictions. By combining liquidity, engagement, and market insights, Tadle transforms illiquid market assets and data into tradeable opportunities for users. Tadle isn’t just another rewards platform, it’s a data-driven trading powerhouse, built for projects optimizing incentives and traders maximizing value in the evolving market.

What is the Tadle Points Marketplace?

The Points Marketplace allows users to buy and sell points with one another, providing a solution for those seeking liquidity before a project’s token issuance. After purchasing, traders can relist points to earn potential profits with each sale. Sellers receive their funds instantly upon trade completion, and if orders go unfulfilled, sellers can cancel at any time and retrieve their collateral, if applicable. Early point creators earn a bonus: a percentage of all future transactions involving the points they introduced to the market.

What exactly are points?

Before issuing tokens, Web3 projects often run initiatives to boost user engagement, awarding gems for participation. These gems can potentially be exchanged for corresponding tokens in the future.

What is the Missions Marketplace?

The Mission Marketplace allows users to complete quests from different projects, engaging in meaningful interactions with their ecosystem that will be rewarded with Tadle Gems upon completion that you can trade for USDT.

What exactly are Tadle Gems?

Unlike points that are issued by a project, to be either traded on Tadle or held on to for an airdrop, Tadle Gems are issued by Tadle to reward users for completing quests on the Mission Marketplace and they can be traded instantly for USDT.

What is the Tadle Odds Marketplace?

The Odds Marketplace enables users to place predictions on key project milestones and market events, turning insights into potential rewards.

How does Tadle Odds work?

Users can predict project-related events, If their predictions are correct, they earn rewards based on market outcomes. Predictions can be placed on project growth, token launch dates, TVL milestones, branding, performance, metrics, ecosystem milestones, governance decisions, and more, allowing traders to capitalize on real market events.
